    A meter you can explain

    Heavier topics count for more than lighter ones. The score is plain arithmetic, so students can trust it and faculty can check it. AI helps with recommendations and mentoring, but never decides your score.

    Dream Meter % = (weight of ticked lessons ÷ weight of all lessons) × 100

    Example: 62 of 100 weight points ticked is 62%.

    Questions students ask

    Is DreamPath AI free?

    Yes. It is a college project built to help students plan their learning, so there are no plans or payments.

    Where do the videos come from?

    Each lesson links to a YouTube search for the topic, and faculty can replace any lesson with a specific, verified video. Lessons marked Verified have been reviewed.

    How is the Dream Meter calculated?

    Every lesson has a weight from 1 to 10. Your meter is the weight you have ticked divided by the total weight of your roadmap, as a percentage.

    Does a higher package make my roadmap longer?

    Yes. A higher target unlocks more advanced lessons, so your roadmap and your meter reflect what that goal actually asks for.

    Do I need an AI key to use the mentor?

    No. Without a key the mentor answers from built-in rules using your own progress. With a key it gives richer, conversational answers.
