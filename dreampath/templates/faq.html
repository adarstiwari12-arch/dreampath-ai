Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about preparing for your dream placement with DreamPath AI.

What is DreamPath AI and how does it help me?

DreamPath AI is a structured skill and career intelligence platform designed for engineering students. Instead of searching across hundreds of random YouTube playlists or tutorials, you choose your branch (CSE, ECE, EEE, Mechanical, or Civil) and your dream salary target in LPA (Lakhs Per Annum). The platform organizes your study journey into 5 ordered phases and calculates your placement readiness using the weighted Dream Meter.

How is the Dream Meter calculated?

The Dream Meter uses transparent arithmetic rather than opaque guessing:

Dream Meter % = (Sum of weight of completed lessons ÷ Sum of weight of all lessons in your tier) × 100

Each lesson is assigned a weight from 1 to 10 points based on its importance in recruiter interviews (e.g. Data Structures carries 10 points, while basic tools carry 3 points). Ticking a heavy lesson advances your score faster because it delivers greater placement leverage.

What do the package tiers mean?

Your target LPA maps directly to one of four curriculum depth tiers:

  • Starter (3 - 6 LPA): Core engineering fundamentals, essential programming/tools, and standard aptitude needed for service and core sector recruiters.
  • Growth (6 - 12 LPA): Intermediate subjects, database administration, web/embedded projects, and product-focused problem solving.
  • Pro (12 - 25 LPA): High-level system design, operating systems internals, advanced project development, and competitive coding.
  • Elite (25 - 60+ LPA): Distributed systems, research-level foundations, cloud microservices, and specialized domain mastery.

When you raise your target LPA in your settings, deeper lessons unlock automatically.

Can I change my stream or target package later?

Yes, at any time! Visit Change plan in your dashboard or account menu. You can slide your target LPA or switch your stream. Your completed progress in each stream is saved independently in the database, so switching back will never erase your earlier ticks.

How does the AI Mentor work?

The AI Mentor looks at your current stream, selected target package, completed phases, and remaining high-weight lessons. It answers questions about what to study next, how to manage your daily hours, and how to prepare for placement interviews.

If an LLM API key is configured by the deployment, answers are streamed using modern generative language models. If not, the platform seamlessly uses intelligent rule-based algorithms based on your actual database progress, ensuring you never receive a broken response or hallucinated curriculum.

What does the "Verified" badge on a lesson mean?

A "Verified" badge signifies that the lesson link, curriculum syllabus, and expected learning outcome have been personally reviewed and confirmed by our faculty members and subject matter coordinators.
